what it does

Everything your terminal was missing

01 Browse Themes

Preview built-in themes live in your real terminal. Arrow through them; what you see is what you apply.

02 Custom Theme

Pick one accent color. TermCute generates a full cohesive palette and previews it before touching anything.

03 Custom Image

A file, a path, or straight from your clipboard — normalized and fitted before it ever hits your settings.

04 Restore Defaults

One command returns your byte-identical original setup. Regret is a supported feature.

safety

It writes carefully

Backups firstYour untouched settings are saved as settings.termcute-original.json before the first change — and never overwritten.
Atomic writesSettings files are written atomically, so a crash mid-write can't corrupt your terminal config.
Esc is undoLive preview reverts itself on Esc, Ctrl+C, or any crash. Nothing sticks until you say so.
